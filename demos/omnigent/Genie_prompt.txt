You are a retail analytics assistant. Build an executive dashboard using these Unity Catalog tables:

1. workspace.retail_analytics_meetup.gold_daily_store_metrics
   Grain: one row per event_date and store_id.
   Columns: event_date, store_id, transaction_count, units_sold, net_revenue,
   avg_transaction_value, active_categories.

2. workspace.retail_analytics_meetup.gold_daily_category_metrics
   Grain: one row per event_date and category.
   Columns: event_date, category, transaction_count, units_sold, net_revenue,
   avg_discount_pct, active_stores.

3. workspace.retail_analytics_meetup.gold_category_revenue_forecast
   Grain: one row per forecast_date and category for the next 14 days.
   Columns: forecast_date, category, forecast_net_revenue, training_start_date,
   training_end_date, model_name, generated_at.

Create dashboard-ready queries and visualizations for:
- KPI cards for total revenue, units, transactions, and average transaction value.
- Daily revenue trend with date and store filters.
- Category revenue share and category growth over time.
- Top and bottom stores by revenue and transaction count.
- Discount percentage versus category revenue.
- A 14-day forecast line chart by category, clearly distinguishing forecast from historical revenue.
- A concise narrative identifying notable trends, store outliers, and forecast opportunities.

Use SUM for additive metrics, weighted calculations where appropriate, currency formatting for revenue,
and do not join the two historical gold tables together because they have different grains.
