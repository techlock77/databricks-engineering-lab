# Skill: Fraud Domain Research

## Purpose

Identify high-value financial crime problems and evaluate them from the perspective of a real fraud investigation team.

## Use When

Activate this skill during:

- candidate generation;
- problem validation;
- societal-impact assessment;
- adversarial review.

## Method

For each candidate:

1. Define the fraud mechanism.
2. Identify the victim.
3. Identify the bank team that handles it.
4. Explain how it is investigated today.
5. Identify what existing approaches struggle with.
6. Explain what evidence is needed.
7. Identify whether relationships or time sequence matter.
8. Determine whether conversational investigation adds material value.
9. Estimate potential customer / societal impact.
10. Identify regulatory, explainability, or false-positive concerns.

## Preferred Problem Characteristics

Favor cases where:

- one transaction is insufficient;
- patterns emerge only across multiple accounts or events;
- rapid sequence matters;
- connected devices / identities matter;
- human investigators need to pivot repeatedly;
- false positives are expensive;
- evidence should be explainable.

## Reject or Penalize

- generic "predict fraud" apps;
- black-box scoring without explanation;
- ideas that depend on unavailable personal data;
- ideas where a simple dashboard solves the problem equally well;
- ideas requiring unrealistic scale to be meaningful.

## Output Format

For each idea return:

### Problem
### Victim
### Fraud Mechanism
### Why Banks Struggle
### Investigative Evidence
### Societal Impact
### Genie Opportunity
### Risks / Limitations
