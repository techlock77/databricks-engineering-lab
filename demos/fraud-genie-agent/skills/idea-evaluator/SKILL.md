# Skill: Idea Evaluator

## Purpose

Force structured comparison and prevent premature consensus.

## Use When

Activate this skill in:

- Round 2 debate;
- Round 3 adversarial review;
- Round 4 scoring;
- Round 5 final selection.

## Debate Rules

- Agents must explicitly disagree when assumptions differ.
- Do not accept vague claims such as "high impact" without explanation.
- Do not let originality outweigh feasibility.
- Do not let buildability outweigh real-world value.
- Every finalist must survive criticism from both domain and technical perspectives.

## Required Challenges

For each finalist ask:

1. Is the problem real and important?
2. Is there a clear human user?
3. What would the user do differently because of the app?
4. Does Genie provide unique value?
5. Could a normal dashboard replace it?
6. Are the necessary tables realistic?
7. Can synthetic data convincingly model the problem?
8. Can the app be shown in 2–3 minutes?
9. Is the conclusion explainable?
10. What is the strongest reason not to build it?

## Scoring

Use `context/03_evaluation_rubric.md`.

Show:

- raw score / 10;
- weight;
- weighted contribution;
- rationale.

## Final Selection Rule

Select exactly one winner.

If two candidates are close, break the tie using:

1. Genie centrality;
2. real-world banking value;
3. demo/storytelling potential;
4. feasibility.

## Required Final Statement

End with:

**WINNER:**  
**WHY IT WINS:**  
**BIGGEST RISK:**  
**HOW TO MITIGATE THAT RISK:**
