# Skill: Demo Designer

## Purpose

Turn the selected application into a coherent, memorable live Genie investigation.

## Use When

Activate only after Round 5 selects one application.

## Step 1 — Create One Synthetic Incident

Define:

- suspicious event;
- account;
- amount;
- timestamp;
- channel;
- device;
- connected accounts;
- historical behavior.

Hide the full fraud pattern at the start.

## Step 2 — Create the Landing Alert

Show only enough information to make the investigator curious.

Example:

- high-risk activity;
- account;
- amount;
- number of transactions;
- risk score;
- Investigate with Genie button.

## Step 3 — Build the Investigation Sequence

Generate 8–12 live prompts.

Required progression:

1. Why was this account flagged?
2. How does this differ from normal behavior?
3. Are other accounts connected?
4. Trace the movement of funds.
5. Are similar patterns present elsewhere?
6. Which customers may be affected?
7. What behavior should investigators examine further?
8. Why could traditional transaction rules miss this?
9. What is the blast radius?
10. What should investigators prioritize next?

## Step 4 — Create the Reveal

The initial event should become a larger pattern.

Example:

1 account
→ 23 accounts
→ 8 potential victims
→ 3 devices
→ 2 external destinations
→ $184K linked movement

## Step 5 — Prove Genie Matters

Add questions such as:

- Compare this pattern with similar incidents from the last 90 days.
- What characteristics are common?
- Which relationships would be difficult to see if transactions were reviewed individually?

## Step 6 — Human Impact

Include at least two questions tied to customer harm.

Examples:

- How many customers may be financially exposed?
- How long did this pattern continue before detection?

Avoid demographic profiling.

## Step 7 — Build the 2–3 Minute Script

For each demo beat provide:

- What I say
- What I show
- What I type into Genie
- What Genie should discover
- Why it matters

## Final Required Section

### LIVE GENIE PROMPTS

Return the exact prompts in order.

The final prompt must be:

> What would we have missed if we investigated only the original transaction?
