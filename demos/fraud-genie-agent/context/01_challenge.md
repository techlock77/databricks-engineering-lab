# Challenge Context

## Objective

Build a Databricks Genie-powered application where Genie is central to the user experience.

The application should solve a real-world financial-industry problem involving fraud prevention, fraud investigation, customer protection, or financial safety.

The preferred framing is not:

> "Can AI predict fraud?"

The preferred framing is:

> "Can Genie help an investigator understand what happened, why it is suspicious, who else is connected, who may be affected, and what evidence should be reviewed next?"

## Desired Competition Characteristics

The final idea should:

- solve a real business problem;
- be understandable to a nontechnical audience;
- be implementable as a working prototype;
- use Databricks Free Edition-compatible capabilities;
- make Genie indispensable to the experience;
- support strong storytelling;
- demonstrate measurable customer or societal impact.

## Core Design Test

Ask:

> If Genie were removed, would the app lose a major part of its value?

If the answer is "no," the idea is too dashboard-centric.

## Preferred User Journey

Alert or unusual event
→ ask Genie why
→ examine behavioral context
→ discover relationships
→ follow the money
→ identify a pattern
→ identify potential victims
→ quantify blast radius
→ prioritize human investigation
