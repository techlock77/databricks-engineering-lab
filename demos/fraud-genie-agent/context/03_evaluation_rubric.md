# Evaluation Rubric

Score each finalist from 1 to 10 in every category.

| Category | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Real-world banking value | 20% |
| Potential societal impact | 15% |
| Genie centrality | 20% |
| Originality | 10% |
| Data feasibility | 10% |
| Databricks Free Edition feasibility | 10% |
| Explainability | 5% |
| Demo / storytelling potential | 10% |

## Scoring Guidance

### Real-World Banking Value — 20%

10:
- solves a problem banks actively care about;
- has a clear user;
- improves a real investigation or prevention workflow.

1:
- mostly academic or hypothetical.

### Societal Impact — 15%

10:
- could materially reduce customer harm;
- has a clear connection to financial safety.

1:
- mostly internal optimization with little customer impact.

### Genie Centrality — 20%

10:
- investigation depends on conversational exploration;
- Genie connects evidence across curated tables;
- removing Genie fundamentally weakens the product.

1:
- Genie is just a chatbot beside a dashboard.

### Originality — 10%

10:
- distinctive framing;
- avoids generic fraud-score dashboards.

### Data Feasibility — 10%

10:
- realistic synthetic data can be generated;
- relationships can be modeled cleanly.

### Free Edition Feasibility — 10%

10:
- can be demonstrated with lightweight data volumes and supported Databricks components.

### Explainability — 5%

10:
- conclusions can be tied to observable evidence.

### Demo Potential — 10%

10:
- strong reveal;
- easy to understand;
- works in a 2–3 minute narrative.

## Weighted Score Formula

For each criterion:

`weighted_points = score / 10 * weight`

Total score is out of 100.

The final output must show both raw score and weighted contribution.
