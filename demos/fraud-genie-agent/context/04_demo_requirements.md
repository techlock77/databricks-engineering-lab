# Live Demo Requirements

The final app must support a story-driven live investigation.

## Required Narrative

Suspicious event
→ Why was it flagged?
→ What changed?
→ Who is connected?
→ Follow the money
→ Find the pattern
→ Identify possible victims
→ Explain why old approaches may miss it
→ Quantify blast radius
→ Recommend what the investigator should review next

## Required Demo Properties

- 2–3 minutes total.
- First 20 seconds establish the incident.
- Middle section contains a meaningful reveal.
- Final 20–30 seconds connect the app to customer and societal impact.
- Questions must sound natural.
- Answers must be evidence-based.
- Do not claim legal guilt.
- Do not let the model autonomously freeze accounts or take punitive action.

## Strong Reveal Example

Initial view:

- 1 suspicious account

After investigation:

- 23 connected accounts
- 8 potential victims
- $184,000 in linked suspicious movement
- 3 shared devices
- 2 external destinations

## Required Closing Question

The demo should end with:

> What would we have missed if we investigated only the original transaction?

The answer should explain why connected, conversational analysis reveals evidence that isolated transaction review may miss.
